# Community Guidelines & Safety

At Dodo Space Game, we prioritize a safe and welcoming environment for all explorers.

## 1. Respect & Conduct
*   Be kind. Harassment, hate speech, and toxicity are strictly prohibited.
*   Our **Live AI Watcher** monitors chat 24/7. Violations result in instant warnings or bans.

## 2. Fair Play
*   Cheating, exploiting bugs, or using unauthorized third-party software (except verified Platinum Tools) is banned.
*   Report bad behavior using the in-game command: `/report <username> <reason>`.

## 3. Wiki & Content Contribution
*   We encourage you to document your discoveries on the Wiki!
*   Please ensure all information is accurate and spoiler-tagged where appropriate.
*   Do not vandalize community pages.

## 4. Account Security (Platinum Safety)
*   Never share your password or Platinum Token.
*   Official support will NEVER ask for your credit card number in chat.
*   All transactions are secured via **Password Bank** technology.

*Failure to follow these rules may result in account suspension via the Moderation System.*
