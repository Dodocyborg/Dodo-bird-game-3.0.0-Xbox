# Marketing Strategy: "Endgame & Beyond" (Update 6.0)

## Phase 1: The "Platinum" Rebranding
*   **Goal**: Establish trust in the new economy.
*   **Action**: Highlight "Platinum at Work" and "Password Bank" security in all trailers.
*   **Tagline**: "Play Secure. Play Infinite."

## Phase 2: Community Power
*   **Goal**: Drive engagement through Wiki and Socials.
*   **Action**: "Wiki Wednesdays" - Reward top contributors with Dodo Credits.
*   **Feature**: In-game "News Feed" pulling top community posts.

## Phase 3: The Endgame Push
*   **Goal**: Monetize high-level players.
*   **Action**: Showcase "Endgame Items" (Swords, Ships) that require real investment or massive grind.
*   **Offer**: Limited time "E-Gift Card" bundles (Buy $50, Get $10 Bonus).

## Phase 4: Influencer Activation
*   **Target**: Streamers who enjoy infinite procedural games (e.g., Minecraft, No Man's Sky creators).
*   **Pitch**: "The game that never ends, with a chat bot that talks back."
